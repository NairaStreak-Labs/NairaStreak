Non-Custodial Transparency

Your money never enters NairaStreak

We are not a bank, investment platform, or crypto wallet. Your money stays in your personal bank accounts, cooperative, or piggybank. NairaStreak is the psychological habit engine that keeps you disciplined.

🛡️

Zero Custody of Funds

NairaStreak cannot deposit, withdraw, transfer, or touch your money. We don't ask for BVN, NIN, or card numbers.

💾

Private & Offline-First

Your data stays on your device with local storage and optional JSON backups. Works 100% offline via PWA.

🚫

No Loans, No Speculation

We will never try to sell you crypto, payday loans, or high-risk schemes. Our only mission is your personal savings consistency.

🔔

Smart Non-Intrusive Habits

Timely notifications remind you during the day, then automatically stop once you log today's progress.
