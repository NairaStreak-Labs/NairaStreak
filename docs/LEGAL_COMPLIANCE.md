## 2. Privacy & Data Protection Compliance (NDPR / GDPR)

### 2.1 Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) Compliance
NairaStreak Labs adheres to the fundamental data protection principles stipulated by the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and NDPR:
1. **Lawfulness & Consent**: User email and data are collected strictly upon voluntary sign-up and explicit user consent.
2. **Purpose Limitation**: Data is processed exclusively to maintain the user's savings goals and progress calculations.
3. **Data Minimization**: We do not request bank verification numbers (BVN), National Identification Numbers (NIN), debit card details, or banking passwords.
4. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)**: Users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated savings entries at any time via Settings or written request.
5. **Data Portability**: Users can export their entire history in machine-readable JSON format at any time.
