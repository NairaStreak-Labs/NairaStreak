# User Personas — NairaStreak Labs

> **Focus**: Target savers seeking habit consistency, accountability, and clarity.

---

## Persona 1: Amina the Rent Planner
- **Demographics**: 26, Junior Product Marketer, Lagos.
- **Income**: Monthly salary (₦280,000/month).
- **Core Challenge**: Rent in Lagos is paid in an annual lump sum (₦800,000). Every year, scrambling to gather funds causes extreme anxiety.
- **Savings Goal**: ₦800,000 over 10 months (~₦2,666/day or ~₦18,500/week).
- **Behavior & Habits**:
  - Uses commercial bank apps for direct transfers.
  - Tends to dip into regular savings unless visually tracking a strict streak.
  - Values privacy and wants zero complicated setup.
- **How NairaStreak Labs Helps**:
  - Breaks the terrifying ₦800k figure into a manageable daily commitment.
  - Streak tracking prevents casual impulse spending.
  - Confetti milestones at 25% and 50% keep motivation high months before rent is due.

## Persona 2: Chukwudi the Freelance Creator
- **Demographics**: 24, Freelance UI Designer & Frontend Developer, Port Harcourt.
- **Income**: Irregular, project-based payments (varying between ₦150k and ₦600k per month).
- **Core Challenge**: Income arrives irregularly; needs to build an emergency fund of ₦500,000 so dry seasons don't derail living expenses.
- **Savings Goal**: ₦500,000 over 6 months (~₦2,770/day).
- **Behavior & Habits**:
  - Tech-savvy, uses dark mode, values fast PWA performance and offline access.
  - Wants to log custom irregular amounts whenever client payments land.
- **How NairaStreak Labs Helps**:
  - Flexible logging allows logging both daily targets and custom lump sums.
  - Dynamic pace projection adjusts estimated completion dates whenever extra savings are logged.

## Persona 3: Tolu the Career Builder
- **Demographics**: 22, Final Year University Student, Ibadan.
- **Income**: Monthly allowance + tutoring gig (₦50,000/month).
- **Core Challenge**: Wants to buy a refurbished workstation laptop (₦250,000) before graduation to start freelancing.
- **Savings Goal**: ₦250,000 over 8 months (~₦1,000/day).
- **Behavior & Habits**:
  - Mobile-first user on an Android smartphone with intermittent internet connectivity.
  - Easily discouraged by complex investment or loan apps.
- **How NairaStreak Labs Helps**:
  - Works 100% offline via PWA caching; data persists locally and syncs smoothly.
  - Micro-contributions (₦1,000/day) feel achievable.
  - Calendar streak view turns saving into an engaging daily routine.

## Persona 4: Fatima the Disciplined Micro-Entrepreneur
- **Demographics**: 32, Artisan & Boutique Shop Owner, Abuja.
- **Income**: Daily cash and digital transfer inflow.
- **Core Challenge**: Mixing personal savings with business operating cash. Needs a dedicated personal tracker to ensure personal savings targets remain untouched.
- **Savings Goal**: ₦1,000,000 expansion & emergency buffer over 12 months.
- **Behavior & Habits**:
  - Accustomed to traditional *Ajo* / *Esusu* contribution routines.
  - Wants a clean, reliable, digital mirror of her savings discipline.
- **How NairaStreak Labs Helps**:
  - Simple daily logging matches her daily retail closing routine.
  - Daily reminders at 8:00 PM nudge her to log before sleeping.
  - Non-custodial nature means no risk to her working capital.
